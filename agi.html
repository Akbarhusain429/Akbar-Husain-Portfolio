01 / THE BIG IDEA A NEW KIND OF POSSIBILITY
*

So, what
is AGI?

Imagine an intelligence that doesn’t just do one thing well — it can learn to do many things.

Artificial General Intelligence, or AGI, is the idea of an AI system that could understand, learn, and apply knowledge across a wide range of tasks at a human-like level. Rather than being trained for only one job, it could use what it learns in one area to solve problems in another.

i

A useful distinction: AGI is still a goal and an active area of research. The AI tools we use today are impressive, but they are not AGI.

A DEEPER LOOK AT THE BIG IDEA

Explore AGI.

AGI is the idea of a machine with broad, adaptable intelligence: one that could learn and reason across different subjects and unfamiliar situations. It has not been established as a reality today. Explore the questions below to understand what it might mean — and what remains uncertain.

This guide explains a research concept. It is not a live AI chatbot or a claim that AGI already exists.
